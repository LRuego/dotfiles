# Quickshell Future Ideas

## Global Hotplug Notification Service
Create a generic `HotplugService.qml` that listens for devices across multiple udev subsystems.
- Command: `udevadm monitor --udev --subsystem-match=block --subsystem-match=sound --subsystem-match=bluetooth`
- Logic: Parse the output for "add" and "remove" events.
- Notification: Trigger a Quickshell popup notification saying "USB Drive Connected", "Bluetooth Headset Disconnected", etc.
